The deliverable

One document, written by the person who is accountable for it.

A consultancy will give you four reports from four firms and leave the reconciling to you. The contradictions between them are the part that matters, and they are exactly the part nobody owns.

We hire the specialists — surveyor, civil engineer, forester, land-use attorney, cost consultant, board-certified safety professional — and then do the work of turning what they produce into a single position with a recommendation on the front of it. If two experts disagree, that disagreement is named and resolved in the document rather than left for you to notice.

Discretion is assumed. Some of this work is about people, and some clients would rather not be a reference. Nothing is published or named without written consent.

What is in it

  • 01The recommendation, first. One page. If you read nothing else you know what we think and why.
  • 02What we found. The facts, sourced, with the specialists who produced them named.
  • 03What it costs. A model you can interrogate, not a single number.
  • 04What could go wrong. Ranked, priced, each with a mitigation and an owner.
  • 05What we do not know. The open questions and what it would cost to close them.
  • 06What to do next. In order, with who does it.

What you do with it

The document is yours. So is the decision.

An estimate written by the company that wants to build it is a sales document with numbers in it. That is not a criticism of contractors — it is just what the incentive produces. Ours is written before anyone is bidding, which is the only point at which the number can be honest.

There is no clause that steers you back to us. Our fee is the same whether you build it with Strongpoint Build, build it with somebody else, or decide not to build it. Telling an owner their project does not pencil is a legitimate outcome of this work, and it happens.

Three honest endings

  • 01Shop it. Take the scope and estimate to three contractors. They are now bidding the same thing, so the numbers can actually be compared — which is usually worth more than the fee.
  • 02Sit on it. Dream projects are allowed to stay dreams for a while. The document does not expire, and it tells you what would have to change for the answer to change.
  • 03Hand it to Build. If you want it managed, Strongpoint Build is the adjacent operation and already has the scope. No re-pricing, no restart.

Engagement

Independent of the company that builds — and we will say so in writing.

Strongpoint Build is our adjacent operation and an Oregon CCB-licensed general contractor. We are deliberately not the same entity, because advising someone on whether to do a project is not a job for the people who would be paid to do it.

Where a project would touch both, you are told before you engage and you decide. If the honest answer is that you need an independent party we have no relationship with, that is the answer you will get.
